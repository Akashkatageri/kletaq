# Kletaq Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

Your privacy is paramount at Kletaq. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, protect, and use your information.

---

### 1. Information Collected
We collect information required to personalize your academic experience:
- **Account details**: Google email address, display name, profile photo URL.
- **Profile metadata**: Chosen unique username, engineering branch, current semester.
- **Study metrics**: XP, study session durations, completed tasks, and streak history.

### 2. Google Account Information
When you sign in via Google Authentication, we securely receive your primary email, full name, and avatar image. We do not access your contacts, Google Drive files, or emails.

### 3. Usage Analytics
We collect anonymous performance metrics to optimize study session timers, syllabus rendering speeds, and app stability.

### 4. Device Information
Basic device parameters (Android SDK version, device model) are logged to ensure layout compatibility across screen sizes.

### 5. Data Storage & Firestore
Your profile data is stored securely using Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore with standard encryption in transit and at rest.

### 6. Third-Party Services
Kletaq uses Google Play Services and Firebase services (Auth, Cloud Firestore) to handle identity management and data persistence.

### 7. Data Security
We enforce strict Firestore Security Rules to guarantee that personal academic records and usernames are isolated and protected against unauthorized access.

### 8. User Rights
You have the right to request export or complete deletion of your user profile and study history at any time by contacting support.

### 9. Contact Information
For privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact `kletaq.dev@gmail.com`.
