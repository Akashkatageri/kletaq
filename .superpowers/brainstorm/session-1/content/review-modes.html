How Should You Practice & Test Your Recall?

Once your room photos are snapped and the AI anchors the derivation steps to your loci, how do you practice in the hours before the exam?

1

Mental Walk & Reveal (Fastest & Patrick Jane Style)

App shows your photo of Locus #1 (e.g. Your Door). You close your eyes, visualize the story, write the formula on your desk paper, and tap "Reveal" to verify.

• Why it works: Zero typing friction. Allows speed-running a 10-step derivation in 60 seconds before entering the exam hall.
2

Finger Scratchpad / Canvas (Write directly on screen)

App shows the room photo with a blank scratchpad area where you can scribble the formula or code line with your finger before revealing the AI answer.

• Why it works: Muscle memory for math symbols, but takes slightly longer per step.
3

Loci Drag & Match Puzzle (Active Ordering)

App shuffles the derivation steps. You see your room layout and must drag each mathematical equation to its correct physical room anchor.

• Why it works: Perfect for checking if you know the exact sequence without skipping critical derivation steps.
