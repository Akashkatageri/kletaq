AR Scan vs. Pinned Room Photos

Why full 3D AR sounds cool in theory, but pinned room photos win in real student life.

3D / ARCore Room Scan
  • ✗ Location Locked: You can only walk through it while physically standing in that room pointing your camera. You cannot revise on the college bus, library, or corridor.
  • ✗ Compatibility Issues: ARCore fails or is unsupported on many budget/mid-tier Android phones (Redmi, Realme, Vivo).
  • ✗ Heavy Battery & Lag: Heats up the phone and drains 30% battery in 15 minutes.
  • ✗ APK Bloat: Adds 40MB+ of 3D rendering engines.
Pinned Room Photos (Patrick Jane Style)
  • ✓ Revise Anywhere: Pull out your phone in the exam corridor, library, or hostel bed and mental-walk through your room in 60 seconds.
  • ✓ 100% Phone Compatibility: Runs lightning fast on every Android phone without ARCore requirements.
  • ✓ True Mental Training: Seeds your brain with real spatial anchors so when you write on paper in the exam, your mind recalls the room effortlessly.
  • ✓ 0 MB Extra Bloat: Pure lightweight Jetpack Compose.
