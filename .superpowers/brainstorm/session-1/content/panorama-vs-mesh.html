How a "Scan Once, View Anywhere" Palace Works on Android

You want a single capture that turns into a reusable virtual space you can explore anywhere. Here is the technical reality:

360° Panorama / Virtual Tour (Gyroscope)

How it works: You do a 1-time panoramic sweep of your room. The app stitches it into an interactive 360° room (like Google StreetView).

  • ✓ View Anywhere: Spin around your room with your finger or by tilting your phone (gyroscope) while sitting on the bus or in bed.
  • ✓ Floating 3D Pins: Pins #1, #2, #3 float over your door, sofa, and desk as you look around.
  • ✓ Works on normal Android phones without needing Apple LiDAR hardware.
Wide Photo + Pinned Objects (Fastest & Cleanest)

How it works: Snap 1 or 2 wide angle photos. Tap 5 objects to drop pins #1 → #5.

  • ✓ Zero Distortion: Clean, crisp photos without messy panoramic stitching seams.
  • ✓ Instant 15-second setup: No slow sweep or calibration required.
  • ✓ Fast speed-runs: You see all steps at a glance without having to physically spin your phone around in a crowded library.
